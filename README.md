# rosette
What I built: Rosette is a guilloche engraver. Banknotes use these looping line patterns as security art, and I thought it fit a payments company better than a shader effect. The cursor tunes a rosette and a click engraves a uniquely serialised note.

Judgment calls: I used no libraries. I spent the time on the gear morphing and detents, the engraving sequence, and the note's typography, and cut sound and export to stay near six hours.

Next: I'd add PNG/SVG export (a plotter-ready SVG would be nice), a gear-click sound, a pressure-sensitive stylus, and a way to share a note by its serial number.
